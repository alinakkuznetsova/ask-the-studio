# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy applies to all orders delivered on or after 1 January 2026.

## Returns window
You can return unworn items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery.

## Items that can't be returned
Earrings are non-returnable for hygiene reasons. The only exceptions are earrings that arrive damaged or faulty, and earrings bought as part of a complete set (see below). Items from our final sale are not eligible for return.

## Returning a set
Sets are sold at a discount, so they can only be returned as a complete set. Return every piece, including the earrings, unworn and in the original packaging, within 30 days of delivery, and we'll refund the set price. We can't accept the return of individual pieces from a set.

## How to start a return
Email hello@parurelondon.com with your order number and the items you'd like to return. We'll reply within two working days with your return instructions.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged or faulty, in which case we'll send a prepaid label. We recommend a tracked service, as we can't refund items lost on their way back to us.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 working days of your return being received and approved. Original delivery charges are not refunded.

## Returning a gift
If you received a Parure piece as a gift, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for store credit. Email hello@parurelondon.com with the order number or the gift receipt included in the parcel. The person who bought the gift won't be told about the return, and the credit is sent to you as a gift card by email.

## Damaged or faulty items
If your piece arrives damaged or faulty, email us within 14 days of delivery with a photo, and we'll offer a replacement or full refund, including delivery costs.
