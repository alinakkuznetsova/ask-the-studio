# Warranty and Repairs

## Our 12-month warranty
Every piece comes with a 12-month warranty against manufacturing faults, such as a broken clasp, a stone coming loose or the plating flaking under normal wear.

## What isn't covered
- Normal wear and tear, including scratches and gradual softening of the finish
- Damage from accidents, bending or misuse
- Damage to pearls from water, perfume or chemicals
- Lost stones or pearls where the setting has been damaged

## How to make a claim
Email hello@parurelondon.com with your order number and a photo of the issue. If your claim is approved, we'll repair or replace the piece free of charge, including postage.

## Repairs outside warranty
We currently don't offer paid repairs outside the warranty period.
