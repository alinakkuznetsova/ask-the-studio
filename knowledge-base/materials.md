# Materials and Skin Sensitivity

## Our base metal
Every Parure piece starts with 316L stainless steel, often called surgical-grade steel. It's strong, doesn't rust, and contains over 60% recycled content.

## Gold pieces (SOLAR and MARINE)
Our gold pieces are coated using PVD (physical vapour deposition), which bonds a thin layer of gold-tone coating to the steel. PVD is much harder-wearing than traditional electroplating, so the colour lasts longer and is resistant to tarnishing.

## Silver-tone pieces (LUNAR)
LUNAR pieces are not plated. They're polished 316L stainless steel in its natural silver tone. Because there's no coating, the colour can't wear off. They are not sterling silver.

## Stones and pearls
- **SOLAR:** red agate, a natural stone.
- **MARINE:** freshwater pearls and blue enamel.
- **LUNAR:** star-cut cubic zirconia crystals.

## Sensitive skin
316L stainless steel is hypoallergenic and meets the EU Nickel Directive limits for jewellery, so most people with sensitive skin can wear our pieces without irritation. If you have a severe, diagnosed nickel allergy, please email hello@parurelondon.com before ordering and we'll talk you through it.
