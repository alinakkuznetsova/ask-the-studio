# About Parure London

## Who we are
Parure London is an emerging London jewellery brand. We believe jewellery is a form of self-expression, not just an accessory. We design sculptural, gold-plated pieces inspired by 17th-century parures and release them as drop-based collections of matching rings, earrings, necklaces and bracelets, designed to be worn together or apart.

## Our values
- **Individuality:** jewellery should reflect the wearer, not follow trends.
- **Curation:** pieces are designed to be worn together or apart with intention.
- **Craftsmanship:** sculptural shapes, finished by hand.
- **Consciousness:** responsible materials and certified manufacturing partners.

## How our pieces are made
Our manufacturing partner is a Responsible Jewellery Council (RJC) certified factory. Our pieces are made from 316L stainless steel containing over 60% recycled content, which is 100% recyclable. Gold pieces are finished with energy-efficient PVD plating. Our packaging is cardboard, which is biodegradable and recyclable.

## Our collections
- **SOLAR** (autumn 2025): gold-plated pieces with red agate.
- **MARINE** (spring 2026): gold-plated pieces with blue enamel and freshwater pearls.
- **LUNAR** (autumn 2026): silver-tone stainless steel with star-cut crystals.
