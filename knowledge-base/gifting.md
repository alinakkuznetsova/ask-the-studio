# Gifting and Gift Cards

## Gift wrapping
Add gift wrapping at checkout for £4. Your order arrives in our signature box tied with a ribbon, with a handwritten card if you add a message.

## Gift receipts
Tick "This is a gift" at checkout and we'll leave prices off the packing slip and include a gift receipt.

## Sending directly
You can enter a different delivery address at checkout to send a gift straight to someone.

## Gift cards
Digital gift cards are available from £25 to £250 and are sent by email. They're valid for 24 months and can be used across all collections. Gift cards can't be exchanged for cash.

## Returning a gift
Gift recipients can return unworn items for store credit. See the Returns and Refunds Policy for details.
