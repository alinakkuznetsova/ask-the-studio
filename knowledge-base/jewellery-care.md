# Jewellery Care

## Cleaning
Clean your jewellery with a small amount of mild soap in lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush, focusing on hard-to-reach areas. Rinse if needed and dry with a soft, lint-free cloth.

Don't scrub hard: even PVD gold-plated stainless steel can dull over time. Avoid chemical cleaners and alcohol wipes.

Buff with a microfibre or jewellery polishing cloth to brighten the surface. This won't remove scratches or dents.

## Storage
Store your pieces somewhere dry and cool, away from direct sunlight. Keep them off wood and out of bathrooms or damp areas.

## When to take it off
Remove your jewellery before sweating, swimming, or applying skincare, perfume or hair products. Jewellery isn't made to be worn 24/7, so rotate your pieces.

## Pearls and stones
Natural stones and pearls need extra care. See the Pearl Care guide for MARINE pieces.
