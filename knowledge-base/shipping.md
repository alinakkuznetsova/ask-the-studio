# Shipping and Delivery

## Dispatch
Orders placed before 1pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 1pm, at weekends or on bank holidays are dispatched the next working day.

## UK delivery
- **Standard (Royal Mail Tracked 48):** £3.95, 2 to 3 working days. Free on orders over £100.
- **Express (Royal Mail Tracked 24):** £6.95, next working day if dispatched that day.

## International delivery
- **Europe:** £9.95, 4 to 7 working days.
- **USA and Canada:** £14.95, 5 to 10 working days.
- **Rest of the world:** £19.95, 7 to 14 working days.

International orders may be charged import duties and taxes by the destination country. These are paid by the customer and are not included in our prices.

## Packaging
Every order arrives in our recyclable cardboard box with a cotton pouch. Gift wrapping is available at checkout (see Gifting).

## Tracking
You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order is dispatched.
