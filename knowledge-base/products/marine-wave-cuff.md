# MARINE Wave Cuff

**Price:** £60

## Description
A sculpted cuff shaped like a breaking wave, with a line of deep blue enamel along its crest.

## Materials
PVD gold plating over 316L stainless steel. Hypoallergenic.

## Size
One size. The cuff has an opening at the back so it slips on from the side of the wrist, but it is rigid and can't be adjusted. Please don't bend it.

- Inner width: 58mm
- Opening: 25mm

## Care
The enamel can chip if knocked against hard surfaces. Store it separately in its pouch.
