# Pearl Care

The freshwater pearls in our MARINE collection are natural and softer than our steel. They need more care than the rest of our jewellery.

## Water
Keep pearls out of water. Don't swim, shower or bathe in MARINE pieces. If they get wet, pat them dry straight away with a soft cloth and let them air dry flat.

## Last on, first off
Put your MARINE pieces on after perfume, hairspray and make-up, and take them off before anything else. Chemicals and oils can dull the pearl's surface.

## Cleaning
Wipe pearls gently with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth after wearing. Never use soap, jewellery cleaner or a toothbrush on pearls.

## Storage
Store MARINE pieces flat in their pouch, separately from other jewellery, so harder metals and crystals can't scratch the pearls.
